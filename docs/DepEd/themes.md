# MATATAG Kindergarten Curriculum — Mathematics Topics

---

## 1. Colors

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME I** | KNOWING WHO WE ARE AND OUR FAMILIES |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the value of knowing oneself, one’s physical features, and surroundings. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners demonstrate basic concepts of sorting and identifying visual attributes in their immediate environment. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Identify, group, and sort concrete objects according to color (primary and secondary colors). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 1. I am unique (My body, my clothes, and things around me). |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Identifying and Sorting Colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple, Black, White) |

---

## 2. Shapes

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME I** | KNOWING WHO WE ARE AND OUR FAMILIES |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the physical characteristics and shapes of common objects in the school and home. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners identify, sort, and construct two-dimensional (2D) shapes using manipulative materials. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Identify and describe common two-dimensional shapes (circle, triangle, square, rectangle). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 2. My family and the things we see in our home. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | 2D Shapes in the Environment (Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle) |

---

## 3. Sequencing

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME II** | EXPLORING OUR COMMUNITY |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the concept of order, logical progression, and patterns in daily routines. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners arrange events and patterns in logical sequential order. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Sequence events with 3 to 4 steps (e.g., daily routines, stories, or growth stages); identify and complete repeating patterns. |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 2. Daily routines in our community and school. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Event Sequencing (First, Next, Last) and Repeating Patterns (AB, AAB) |

---

## 4. Addition

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME III** | DISCOVERING OUR SURROUNDINGS |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the concept of combining sets and the meaning of addition. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners perform simple addition involving concrete objects with sums up to 10. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Combine two sets of concrete objects to find the total sum (up to 10). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 1. Living things and gathering objects in nature. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Joining Sets and Basic Addition (Concrete and Pictorial Models up to 10) |

---

## 5. Subtraction

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME III** | DISCOVERING OUR SURROUNDINGS |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the concept of taking away elements from a set and the meaning of subtraction. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners perform simple subtraction using manipulatives and visual representations within 10. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Take away a specific quantity from a given set of objects to find how many are left (within 10). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 2. Caring for animals and sharing natural resources. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Separating Sets and Basic Subtraction (Taking Away within 10) |

---

## 6. Numbers

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME I** | KNOWING WHO WE ARE AND OUR FAMILIES |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand numeral identification, one-to-one correspondence, and cardinal values from 1 to 20. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners count, recognize, write, and represent quantities up to 20 accurately. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Count objects with one-to-one correspondence; read and write numerals 1 to 20; recognize quantity representations. |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 3. Counting members and objects in our household. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Cardinal Numbers 1 to 20, Counting Songs, and Number Representation |

---

## 7. Measurement

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME IV** | CARING FOR OUR WORLD |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand the concept of comparing and measuring attributes using non-standard tools. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners measure and estimate length, mass, and capacity using non-standard units (paper clips, blocks, hand spans). |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Measure and compare objects using non-standard measurement tools (e.g., longer/shorter, heavier/lighter, holds more/holds less). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 2. Exploring materials and resources in our environment. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Non-Standard Measurement (Length, Height, Weight, and Capacity) |

---

## 8. Comparison

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME I** | KNOWING WHO WE ARE AND OUR FAMILIES |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand relative attributes and relationships between two or more physical objects. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners compare and sort objects based on measurable size, length, and quantity. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Compare objects using comparative terms: big/small, long/short, tall/short, heavy/light, more/less/equal. |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 1. Observing physical traits and everyday objects. |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Comparative Concepts (Big/Small, Long/Short, Tall/Short, More/Less/Equal) |

---

## 9. Clock

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **THEME II** | EXPLORING OUR COMMUNITY |
| **CONTENT STANDARD** | The learners understand basic concepts of time, parts of the day, and tell time by the hour on an analog clock. |
| **PERFORMANCE STANDARD** | The learners tell time to the hour and associate specific times with school and daily community activities. |
| **LEARNING COMPETENCIES** | Tell and show time by the hour using an analog clock; identify times associated with daily routines (morning, noon, night). |
| **SUBTHEMES** | 1. A day in our community (Schedules and helpers). |
| **SUGGESTED CONTENTS** | Telling Time by the Hour (Analog Clock Faces and Daily Schedules) |
